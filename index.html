About the author

K. D. Swartz

K. D. Swartz is an Ontario educator and the designer behind Cedar North Educational Resources, where she creates classroom materials, planners, and printables for teachers, homeschoolers, and makers.

Through the Fog grew out of her own experience of betrayal and separation. She wrote the book she needed at the time: part story, part workbook, built from the prompts and exercises that helped her find her footing again.

She lives and works in Timmins, Ontario.
